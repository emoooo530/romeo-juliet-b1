罗密欧与朱丽叶

Romeo and Juliet · B1 中文戏剧朗读本
朗读练习 · Reading Practice
每个人选择一个角色。先读中文，再根据需要打开拼音和英文。 本剧为适合 B1 学习者的现代改写版，重点练习日常对白、情感表达和叙事。
第一幕：舞会 Act I · The Ball
旁白 · Narrator
今晚，凯普莱特家举行盛大的舞会。两个家族已经争斗很多年，但年轻人并不想继续这种仇恨。
Jīnwǎn, Kǎipǔláitè jiā jǔxíng shèngdà de wǔhuì. Liǎng gè jiāzú yǐjīng zhēngdòu hěn duō nián, dàn niánqīngrén bìng bù xiǎng jìxù zhè zhǒng chóuhèn.
Tonight, the Capulets are holding a grand ball. The two families have fought for years, but the young people do not want to continue the hatred.
罗密欧 · Romeo
我本来不想来这里。可是现在，我觉得今晚也许会发生一些特别的事情。
Wǒ běnlái bù xiǎng lái zhèlǐ. Kěshì xiànzài, wǒ juéde jīnwǎn yěxǔ huì fāshēng yìxiē tèbié de shìqing.
I didn't want to come here. But now I feel that something special might happen tonight.
奶妈 · Nurse
朱丽叶，你别一直站在角落里。去跳舞吧！年轻的时候就应该开心一点。
Zhūlìyè, nǐ bié yìzhí zhàn zài jiǎoluò lǐ. Qù tiàowǔ ba! Niánqīng de shíhou jiù yīnggāi kāixīn yìdiǎn.
Juliet, don't stand in the corner all evening. Go dance! You should enjoy yourself while you're young.
朱丽叶 · Juliet
我不是不开心。我只是觉得，大家好像都在假装自己很快乐。
Wǒ bú shì bù kāixīn. Wǒ zhǐshì juéde, dàjiā hǎoxiàng dōu zài jiǎzhuāng zìjǐ hěn kuàilè.
It's not that I'm unhappy. I just feel that everyone is pretending to be happy.
罗密欧 · Romeo
你好。我可以请你跳一支舞吗？
Nǐ hǎo. Wǒ kěyǐ qǐng nǐ tiào yì zhī wǔ ma?
Hello. May I ask you to dance with me?
朱丽叶 · Juliet
当然可以。可是，你叫什么名字？
Dāngrán kěyǐ. Kěshì, nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
Of course. But what's your name?
罗密欧 · Romeo
罗密欧。你呢？
Luómì'ōu. Nǐ ne?
Romeo. And you?
朱丽叶 · Juliet
朱丽叶。等等……你是蒙太古家的人？
Zhūlìyè. Děngděng…… nǐ shì Méngtàigǔ jiā de rén?
Juliet. Wait... You're from the Montague family?
罗密欧 · Romeo
是的。可是如果我早知道你是谁，我可能还是会来。
Shì de. Kěshì rúguǒ wǒ zǎo zhīdào nǐ shì shéi, wǒ kěnéng háishi huì lái.
Yes. But even if I had known who you were, I might still have come.
第二幕：阳台 Act II · The Balcony
旁白 · Narrator
舞会结束后，朱丽叶站在阳台上。罗密欧没有离开，他悄悄走到花园里。
Wǔhuì jiéshù hòu, Zhūlìyè zhàn zài yángtái shàng. Luómì'ōu méiyǒu líkāi, tā qiāoqiāo zǒu dào huāyuán lǐ.
After the ball, Juliet stands on the balcony. Romeo has not left; he quietly walks into the garden.
朱丽叶 · Juliet
为什么偏偏是你？为什么你一定要属于那个家族？
Wèishénme piānpiān shì nǐ? Wèishénme nǐ yídìng yào shǔyú nàge jiāzú?
Why does it have to be you? Why must you belong to that family?
罗密欧 · Romeo
如果名字可以改变，我愿意换一个名字。可是我不能改变自己的心。
Rúguǒ míngzi kěyǐ gǎibiàn, wǒ yuànyì huàn yí gè míngzi. Kěshì wǒ bù néng gǎibiàn zìjǐ de xīn.
If a name could be changed, I would choose another one. But I cannot change my heart.
朱丽叶 · Juliet
我也喜欢你，可是我害怕。我们的家人不会同意。
Wǒ yě xǐhuan nǐ, kěshì wǒ hàipà. Wǒmen de jiārén bú huì tóngyì.
I like you too, but I'm afraid. Our families will never agree.
罗密欧 · Romeo
那我们就一起想办法。至少，让我们自己决定自己的未来。
Nà wǒmen jiù yìqǐ xiǎng bànfǎ. Zhìshǎo, ràng wǒmen zìjǐ juédìng zìjǐ de wèilái.
Then let's find a way together. At least, let us decide our own future.
牧师 · Friar
如果你们真的决定在一起，我愿意帮助你们。但你们必须明白，这不是一个简单的选择。
Rúguǒ nǐmen zhēnde juédìng zài yìqǐ, wǒ yuànyì bāngzhù nǐmen. Dàn nǐmen bìxū míngbai, zhè bú shì yí gè jiǎndān de xuǎnzé.
If you truly decide to be together, I will help you. But you must understand that this is not a simple choice.
第三幕：冲突 Act III · The Conflict
提伯尔特 · Tybalt
罗密欧！你竟然敢来到这里？你忘了我们的家族是什么关系吗？
Luómì'ōu! Nǐ jìngrán gǎn lái dào zhèlǐ? Nǐ wàng le wǒmen de jiāzú shì shénme guānxi ma?
Romeo! How dare you come here? Have you forgotten what our families mean to each other?
罗密欧 · Romeo
我不想和你打架。我们之间的仇恨不是我们自己选择的。
Wǒ bù xiǎng hé nǐ dǎjià. Wǒmen zhījiān de chóuhèn bú shì wǒmen zìjǐ xuǎnzé de.
I don't want to fight you. The hatred between us is not something we chose.
提伯尔特 · Tybalt
少说漂亮话！你以为爱可以解决所有问题吗？
Shǎo shuō piàoliang huà! Nǐ yǐwéi ài kěyǐ jiějué suǒyǒu wèntí ma?
Enough with the pretty words! Do you think love can solve every problem?
奶妈 · Nurse
够了！你们都是年轻人，为什么不能坐下来好好说话？
Gòu le! Nǐmen dōu shì niánqīngrén, wèishénme bù néng zuò xiàlái hǎohāo shuōhuà?
Enough! You're all young. Why can't you sit down and talk calmly?
牧师 · Friar
她说得对。真正困难的不是相爱，而是学会在冲突中保持理智。
Tā shuō de duì. Zhēnzhèng kùnnan de bú shì xiāng'ài, ér shì xuéhuì zài chōngtū zhōng bǎochí lǐzhì.
She is right. The real difficulty is not loving each other, but staying rational during conflict.
第四幕：误会 Act IV · The Misunderstanding
凯普莱特 · Capulet
朱丽叶，你必须和一个我们家族选择的人结婚。这是为了你好。
Zhūlìyè, nǐ bìxū hé yí gè wǒmen jiāzú xuǎnzé de rén jiéhūn. Zhè shì wèile nǐ hǎo.
Juliet, you must marry someone chosen by our family. It is for your own good.
朱丽叶 · Juliet
可是爸爸，你有没有问过我真正想要什么？
Kěshì bàba, nǐ yǒu méiyǒu wèn guò wǒ zhēnzhèng xiǎng yào shénme?
But Father, have you ever asked what I really want?
凯普莱特 · Capulet
我只是希望你过得安全、幸福。
Wǒ zhǐshì xīwàng nǐ guò de ānquán, xìngfú.
I only want you to be safe and happy.
朱丽叶 · Juliet
幸福不能由别人替我决定。
Xìngfú bù néng yóu biérén tì wǒ juédìng.
My happiness cannot be decided by someone else.
牧师 · Friar
现在已经没有多少时间了。我们必须小心地计划下一步。
Xiànzài yǐjīng méiyǒu duōshao shíjiān le. Wǒmen bìxū xiǎoxīn de jìhuà xià yí bù.
There isn't much time left. We must carefully plan our next step.
第五幕：真相 Act V · The Truth
旁白 · Narrator
计划没有按照他们想象的方式进行。消息没有及时送到罗密欧那里，一场悲剧几乎无法避免。
Jìhuà méiyǒu ànzhào tāmen xiǎngxiàng de fāngshì jìnxíng. Xiāoxi méiyǒu jíshí sòng dào Luómì'ōu nàlǐ, yì chǎng bēijù jīhū wúfǎ bìmiǎn.
The plan does not go as they imagined. The message does not reach Romeo in time, and tragedy seems almost impossible to avoid.
罗密欧 · Romeo
如果我早点知道真相，也许一切都会不一样。
Rúguǒ wǒ zǎodiǎn zhīdào zhēnxiàng, yěxǔ yíqiè dōu huì bù yíyàng.
If I had known the truth earlier, perhaps everything would have been different.
朱丽叶 · Juliet
别让我们的故事只剩下悲伤。请记住，我们曾经努力过。
Bié ràng wǒmen de gùshi zhǐ shèng xià bēishāng. Qǐng jìzhu, wǒmen céngjīng nǔlì guò.
Don't let our story become only a story of sadness. Remember that we tried.
凯普莱特 · Capulet
我终于明白了。我们所谓的保护，其实也可能伤害孩子。
Wǒ zhōngyú míngbai le. Wǒmen suǒwèi de bǎohù, qíshí yě kěnéng shānghài háizi.
I finally understand. What we call protection can also hurt our children.
提伯尔特 · Tybalt
我们争斗了这么多年，最后到底得到了什么？
Wǒmen zhēngdòu le zhème duō nián, zuìhòu dàodǐ dédào le shénme?
We have fought for so many years. What did we actually gain?
奶妈 · Nurse
也许现在还不算太晚。你们可以选择停止仇恨。
Yěxǔ xiànzài hái bú suàn tài wǎn. Nǐmen kěyǐ xuǎnzé tíngzhǐ chóuhèn.
Perhaps it is not too late. You can choose to stop the hatred.
牧师 · Friar
爱不能改变过去，但我们可以决定未来。希望这一次，你们真的记住这一点。
Ài bù néng gǎibiàn guòqù, dàn wǒmen kěyǐ juédìng wèilái. Xīwàng zhè yí cì, nǐmen zhēnde jìzhu zhè yìdiǎn.
Love cannot change the past, but we can decide the future. I hope this time you truly remember that.
旁白 · Narrator
两个家族终于停止了争斗。罗密欧与朱丽叶的故事成为一个提醒：仇恨可以持续很多年，但改变可以从今天开始。
Liǎng gè jiāzú zhōngyú tíngzhǐ le zhēngdòu. Luómì'ōu yǔ Zhūlìyè de gùshi chéngwéi yí gè tíxǐng: chóuhèn kěyǐ chíxù hěn duō nián, dàn gǎibiàn kěyǐ cóng jīntiān kāishǐ.
The two families finally stop fighting. Romeo and Juliet's story becomes a reminder: hatred can last for years, but change can begin today.
